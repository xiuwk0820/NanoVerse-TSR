NanoVerse-TSR

Contrastive Learning-Driven Traffic Sign Recognition for robust, fine-grained perception.

PaperCode

Two-stage, semantic-aware recognition

NanoVerse-TSR first localizes small traffic signs with semantic-visual fusion, then recognizes fine-grained categories through contrastive learning between visual features and traffic-rule language.

Small-object detection

RepVL-PAN semantic guidance with a P2 head supports multi-scale signs.

Long-tail recognition

ViT and Rule-BERT contrastive learning trained on 24,715 image-text pairs.

Robust sensing

Evaluated across TT100K, CCTSDB2021, and adverse-weather subsets.
